How to Play

Tap a group of 2 or more same-colored tiles to clear them from the board.

Tip: Bigger groups give exponentially more points! A group of 5 scores 250, but a group of 10 scores 1000.

After clearing, tiles fall down and columns collapse left. The game ends when no groups remain.
