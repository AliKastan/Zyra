How to Play
Tap near the top of the screen to drop a ball.

Balls bounce off pegs and land in scoring zones at the bottom.

Zones award 1x, 2x, 3x, 2x, 1x multipliers (x10 points).

You have 15 balls per round. Aim carefully!
